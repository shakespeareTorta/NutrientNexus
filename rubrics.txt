Your implementation must demonstrate:

Bidirectional Communication (publish/subscribe)
Show two-way data flow between the two entities using ROS 2 topics (and optionally services/actions). Each side must publish at least one stream that the other subscribes to.

State Synchronization (not just motion commands)
Mirror meaningful states across entities (e.g., sensor status, battery/power state, actuator/motor status, errors/warnings, localization quality, operating mode) with real-time/near-real-time responsiveness.

Environmental & Object Interaction
Demonstrate an environment-driven behavior that propagates through the twin system (e.g., obstacle detection/avoidance, dynamic obstacle response, object pushing/transport). The key requirement is that environment-related events do not remain local to only one side.
